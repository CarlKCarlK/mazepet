About mazepet & 10 PRINT

Inspired by the Commodore 64's 10 PRINT, which draws a maze-like picture from random PETSCII diagonals:

10 PRINT CHR$(205.5+RND(1)); : GOTO 10

mazepet repairs that random picture into a solvable maze while trying to preserve its original diagonals.

Enter on the left at the top; exit on the right at the bottom.
