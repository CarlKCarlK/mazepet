About mazepet & 10 PRINT

Inspired by the Commodore 64's 10 PRINT, which draws a maze-like picture from random PETSCII diagonals:

10 PRINT CHR$(205.5+RND(1)); : GOTO 10

mazepet repairs that random picture into a solvable maze while trying to preserve its original diagonals.

Tap or click a little ahead of the line, or drag, to reach the lower-right exit. You don’t need to grab the dot: short jumps follow a clear passage, including bends. Steer clearly at forks. Tap or drag back along your route to erase steps. Scroll outside the maze to move the page.
